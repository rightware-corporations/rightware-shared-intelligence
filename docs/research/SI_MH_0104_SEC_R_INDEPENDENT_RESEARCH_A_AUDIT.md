## 3. Corrections to recommendations
SPIFFE/mTLS, DPoP and Cedar/ReBAC are candidates, not universally non-negotiable implementations. Separate user authentication, workload authentication, delegated authority, tenant/resource authorization and actual effect enforcement. Do not expose raw DB access or privileges to models. Data returned from tools is untrusted; provenance alone does not guarantee factual truth. Typed allowlists and structured argument validation, not shell metacharacter blacklists, are primary enforcement. Audit should be tamper-evident with independent protected retention and access controls; append-only/hash chain alone is not immutable against a privileged writer. Encryption at rest and per-tenant keys do not prevent disclosure by a service entitled to decrypt all tenants. Namespaces/schemas not equivalent to separate physical isolation. Single-use capability needs atomic consumption with effect/idempotency and explicit delegation attenuation, recipient/action/resource/purpose/argument binding and expiry, not simply signing a JWT. Do not invent cryptography. Revocation SLA is a requirement to approve after measuring risk; cannot undo already delivered bytes or all external effects. "Minutes/hours" TTL and "seconds" revocation in report are proposals, not validated requirements.

- Meta Hit@1 train 56/56 (100%), dev 5/20 (25%), diagnostic 3/20 (15%); nDCG@5 train 1.0000, dev .5258, diagnostic .3851. Strong train-to-diagnostic degradation, not proof of one particular causal mechanism. Both learned models underperform hybrid+authority. B4 applies a hard validity/authority filter; others do not, so future comparisons need common candidate eligibility and a separate retrieval-only tier. Meta top1 ineligible 0/40 diagnostic does not mean correct support.
- The learned standardized coefficients on BM25 normalization are negative (-1.2020 text, -2.3623 meta), token overlap negative (-.4014, -1.1264). Correlated features, very few positive pairs, pointwise objective and unseen identifiers are plausible causes; coefficients are not causal importance. LSA is train-fitted lexical latent space, not a pretrained dense semantic encoder. Char TF-IDF uses train vocabulary; novel random identifiers may be poorly represented. Train feature fitting includes all synthetic train docs, including foreign docs; no private data, but next protocol should fit only approved scope and document assumptions.
- B4 diagnostic PT nDCG .6754, EN .8405 (10 each); Meta PT .4310, EN .3393. Tiny correlated bilingual group sample, no general language superiority claim.
- Abstention: dev threshold 0 accepts 40/40 with 35 wrong (87.5% risk); no grid point with positive coverage and zero observed errors. Minimum risk among nonempty dev grid points .50. Frozen threshold 1.01 accepts 0/40 dev and 0/40 diagnostic; risk null/undefined, not demonstrated accuracy. Scores from balanced class weights are not calibrated probabilities. No negative false accept at zero coverage is not evidence of practical safe answering.
- Post-hoc diagnostic only, not new benchmark: literal entity regex N[0-9A-F]{8} intersected with official/current/scoped sources gives 3 candidates for each of 20 positive diagnostic queries and each of 10 unknown diagnostic queries, zero for 10 foreign-only.

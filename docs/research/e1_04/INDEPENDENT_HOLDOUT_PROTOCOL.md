# Independent held-out evaluation — curator protocol R0
**Proposed operational requirements, NOT an existing dataset, reviewed gold, or approval.** 
The curator must not author/adjust the ranker or see provisional training labels and model results while constructing held-out questions. Produce new synthetic or appropriately licensed documents with provenance and permissions; no real customer information. Separate by entity/source, domain/project, question and time, not random row split alone. Include PT/EN, exact identifiers, paraphrases, semantic alternatives, multi-source support, distractors, conflicts, as-of temporal cases, unanswerable and unauthorized-only cases; do not reuse visible question templates/IDs or E1.1B public eval questions. Establish class/language counts and rubric before model comparison, review coverage and diversity rather than choose numbers to inflate results.

Two independent reviewers score authoritative support and contradiction evidence, adjudicate differences, record source spans, grade and class agreement and unresolved cases. Curator must audit semantic overlap with development set, not simply a hash or lexical similarity check. Version and hash corpus, questions, split manifest, rubric and gold; keep plaintext gold and private metadata in a restricted evaluator environment, not in public Git, CI, ChatGPT, Kaggle or train/dev. Publish only approved redacted commitments and aggregate evidence. Keep test questions reserved from tuning, output selection and model development; run once after code/config/model/metrics freeze with logged inputs/output digests and independent evaluator. Deviations require preregistered new version, not silent retries. Include abstention, authority and disclosure separate from retrieval. No gold or hidden evaluations have been produced by this document.

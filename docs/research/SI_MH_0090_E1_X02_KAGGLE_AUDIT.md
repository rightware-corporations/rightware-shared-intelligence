## Critical diagnosis: rank margin is not evidential support
The frozen gate score equals identity_match × property_coverage × (1 - negative_notice) × (0.5 + 0.5 tanh(nonnegative top1-top2 margin)). It is not a calibrated probability. All 60 negative dev queries and all 60 negative diagnostic queries have confidence 0; nevertheless **9 of 19 dev positive-confidence candidates have wrong source IDs** (10 correct); diagnostic positive-confidence 30/30 answerable, 7 incorrect. Thus all-negative rejection does not fix incorrect positive-source acceptance. On dev the minimum nonempty grid risk is 2/8 =25% at threshold .78 (coverage 8/90), with zero negative-query acceptances. Frozen policy chooses 1.01 and accepts 0/90 both dev and diagnostic; risk undefined. No threshold tuning on diagnostic and no retrospective policy alteration.

Concrete dev examples from raw rankings:
- Q text "Indique verba relativo a RW-E50EDB95D8." Incorrect top source asserts data limite and only mentions orçamento in an audit note; confidence .99351. Correct gold source asserts orçamento with its own value.
- "Find the recorded due date of RW-6F29F54616." Top asserts localização and mentions prazo in a note, confidence .60189. Gold asserts deadline.
- "Find the recorded limit of RW-6827DDEF24." Top asserts data limite, gold capacity. Dictionary matching sees capacity term limit/limite inside deadline phrase data limite; phrase ambiguity and mention-versus-assertion conflation. This illustrates a rule-label/representation risk, not proof that every error has the same cause.
Static notebook inspection: concepts() matches every dictionary phrase anywhere in text; feature property_coverage does not differentiate asserted property/value, contextual mention or clause role. The fixed negative_notice only matches the exact phrase "nenhum valor confirmado". Train/dev/diagnostic templates and dictionary aliases derive from same author; many negatives have a literal cue. Need harder paraphrases and contradiction cases, not treating these 120 negatives as general answerability validation.
